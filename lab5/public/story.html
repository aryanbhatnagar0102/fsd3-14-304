CHANGING

America, 1899. The end of the Wild West era has begun. After a robbery goes badly wrong in Blackwater, Arthur Morgan and the Van der Linde gang are forced to flee.

With lawmen and bounty hunters closing in, the gang must rob, steal and fight their way across the heartland of America to survive.

But as the world changes around them, the gang's ideals, loyalty and trust begin to fall apart.
